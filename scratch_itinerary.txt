# Trip Overview & Highlights  
**Vibe** – A sun‑kissed, laid‑back escape where Portuguese heritage meets vibrant beach culture.  
**Best Time** – Late November to early March (dry season, pleasant 25‑28 °C).  
**Key Highlights**  
- Iconic beaches: Baga, Calangute, Palolem, Agonda.  
- Old Goa’s UNESCO‑listed churches & forts.  
- Dolphin‑watching & spice‑plantation tours.  
- Nightlife: beach shacks, clubbing in Tito’s, and laid‑back bars in Panaji.

# Day‑by‑Day Detailed Itinerary  

### Day 1: **Arrival & Baga Beach Bliss**  
- **Morning**  
  - 09:00 AM: Land at Goa Airport.  
  - 10:00 AM: Private transfer to hotel (Taj Exotica).  
  - 11:00 AM: Check‑in & freshen up.  
- **Afternoon**  
  - 12:30 PM: Beachside lunch at *Mum’s Kitchen* (fresh Goan seafood).  
  - 02:00 PM: Relax on Baga Beach, try parasailing or a quick surf lesson.  
- **Evening**  
  - 05:30 PM: Sunset at Baga’s *Sunset Point*.  
  - 07:00 PM: Dinner at *Martin’s Corner* – famous for its vindaloo.  
  - 09:00 PM: Optional stroll along Calangute promenade or light clubbing at *Tito’s*.

### Day 2: **North Goa – Beaches & Nightlife**  
- **Morning**  
  - 08:00 AM: Breakfast at hotel.  
  - 09:30 AM: Drive to Candolim & Anjuna beaches.  
  - 11:00 AM: Explore Anjuna Flea Market (handicrafts, jewelry).  
- **Afternoon**  
  - 01:00 PM: Lunch at *Pousada by the Sea* (Portuguese tapas).  
  - 02:30 PM: Visit Chapora Fort for panoramic views & photo ops.  
  - 04:00 PM: Return to hotel; spa session (optional).  
- **Evening**  
  - 06:30 PM: Sunset cruise on Mandovi River (includes dinner & live music).  
  - 09:30 PM: Nightlife – choose between *Tito’s* (club) or *Bacchus* (bar).

### Day 3: **South Goa – Culture & Nature**  
- **Morning**  
  - 07:00 AM: Early breakfast; depart for Dudhsagar Falls.  
  - 09:00 AM: Jeep safari to the falls; enjoy the mist & waterfalls.  
- **Afternoon**  
  - 12:00 PM: Picnic lunch at the falls (prepared by local guide).  
  - 01:30 PM: Drive to spice plantation – guided tour & tea tasting.  
  - 04:00 PM: Check‑in at *Alila Diwa Goa* (South Goa).  
- **Evening**  
  - 06:30 PM: Sunset at *Agonda Beach* – quiet, less crowded.  
  - 08:00 PM: Dinner at *The Old House* (traditional Goan cuisine).  

### Day 4: **Old Goa & Panaji**  
- **Morning**  
  - 08:00 AM: Breakfast at hotel.  
  - 09:00 AM: Drive to Old Goa – Basilica of Bom Jesus, Se Cathedral, Church of St. Francis of Assisi.  
- **Afternoon**  
  - 12:30 PM: Lunch at *Vinayak Family Restaurant* (local Goan thali).  
  - 02:00 PM: Visit Aguada Fort & lighthouse; stroll along the promenade.  
  - 04:00 PM: Return to Panaji; explore Latin Quarter (Fontainhas).  
- **Evening**  
  - 06:00 PM: Dolphin‑watching cruise from Mandovi River (morning or evening options).  
  - 08:00 PM: Dinner at *Fisherman’s Wharf* (seafood & live music).  
  - 10:00 PM: Relax at hotel lounge or enjoy a quiet beach walk.  

### Day 5: **Departure & Souvenir Shopping**  
- **Morning**  
  - 07:30 AM: Breakfast at hotel.  
  - 09:00 AM: Last stroll on *Palolem Beach* (if staying in South Goa) or *Baga* (North).  
- **Afternoon**  
  - 11:00 AM: Check‑out; transfer to Goa Airport.  
  - 12:30 PM: Flight back to Mumbai (IndiGo AI 149).  
- **Evening**  
  - 02:00 PM: Arrive at Mumbai Airport, end of trip.

# Practical Travel Tips  

| Topic | Advice |
|-------|--------|
| **Currency** | INR. ATMs plentiful at airports & major hotels. |
| **SIM Card** | Pre‑pay 5G (Airtel, Jio) for data and ride‑hailing apps. |
| **Transport** | Use Uber, Ola, or local taxis. For longer stays, rent a scooter (₹500/day) – wear helmet, carry license. |
| **Packing** | Light cotton, swimwear, sunhat, sunglasses, SPF 50 sunscreen, a light jacket for evenings. |
| **Health** | Stay hydrated; avoid raw street food if you have a sensitive stomach. |
| **Safety** | Keep valuables in a hotel safe. Avoid alcohol‑heavy parties at night. |
| **Local Customs** | Dress modestly when visiting churches. Remove shoes before entering temples. |
| **Connectivity** | Free Wi‑Fi in most hotels and cafes; use Google Maps for navigation. |
| **Tipping** | 10–15 % in restaurants, ₹50–₹100 for hotel housekeeping. |
| **Weather** | Pack a light raincoat for unexpected showers (monsoon season Dec–Mar). |

Enjoy your sun‑filled, culturally rich 5‑day escape to Goa from Mumbai! Safe travels.
